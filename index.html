How to create a new narrative page
1
Duplicate the template
Copy template.html and rename it for your customer, e.g. westpac.html
2
Set the accent colour
In the <style> block at the top, set --accent to the customer's brand hex.
3
Fill in the sections
Replace all {{placeholder}} text. Delete any sections you don't need.
4
Open & click through
Open your .html file in a browser. Scroll through it like a narrative deck.
