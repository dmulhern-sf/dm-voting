What the platform cannot tell you
01

Which of the 3,000 actually travel

Melon only knows the people who showed up. Someone who has never logged in is not a low-engagement user — they are invisible.

02

Who is already travelling another way

Bookings made by a consultant over the phone never touch the platform. Those travellers exist, spend money, and look like non-users.

03

Who should be left alone

Site engineers who never travel are sitting in the denominator, dragging the adoption number down and receiving nudges they will resent.

A 40% adoption number is not an engagement problem. It is a data problem wearing an engagement costume.

Complementary, not competing

Mosaic decides what. Data 360 decides who, where and when.

Mosaic is Trevor's AI brain, built on Anthropic, and it is good at ranking what to recommend. What it has never had is a unified profile to aim at, or a governed way to deliver.

  • Mosaic — the recommendation. Which product, which content, what to say next.
  • Data 360 — the context, the audience, the channel and the moment.
  • Bidirectional. Data 360 enriches what Mosaic can see; Mosaic's recommendation comes back as an action.
✓
Data 360 does not replace Mosaic — any more than it replaces Azure or Melon. It gives Mosaic eyes and a delivery system.
