80/20 rule: 80% of visible color must be primary blues. Secondary colors are single accent pops — never two on the same card. Customer accent replaces one secondary slot per page, not a primary blue.

8a — Cards 2-col (Why Now) — product icons, no emoji
The data is already there
Westpac processes 4.2 million transactions daily. Each one is an unanswered question about what this customer needs next.
The window is shrinking
Customers expect relevance in-moment — not a follow-up email 48 hours later. The competitors who get this are already moving.
Regulation is enabling, not blocking
Open banking changes have lowered the data-sharing barrier. First movers lock in the relationship.
AI is ready for production
This is no longer a research project. Enterprise-grade, auditable, explainable AI is shipping today.
