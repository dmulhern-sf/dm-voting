Executive Summary
What Grill'd is trying to achieve — and how Salesforce delivers it

Grill'd has built one of Australia's most values-driven loyalty programs. The ambition in this RFP is to make that program real-time, reacting to what members do, who they support, and what they care about, and delivering the right communication at exactly the right moment. Salesforce is purpose-built for this.

Unified member data
Data 360 combines member preferences, stamp activity, nominated AFL team, charity selection, and channel history into a single real-time profile, the foundation every personalised communication is built on.
Real-time event detection
Streaming Insights evaluate inbound events from TASK POS and external feeds, detecting stamp milestones and AFL game results within milliseconds and firing the right journey automatically.
Multi-channel journey orchestration
Marketing Cloud (Next) delivers personalised push notifications, SMS, and email across both use cases, with Salesforce Flow providing the branching logic that handles every member scenario cleanly.
The two use cases this response covers
Use Case 4 — Mad Bunday Campaign Journey
When a member's nominated AFL team wins on Saturday, a personalised coupon lands in their app that evening — redeemable on Monday at any Grill'd location with a minimum spend. Members with Spoiler Protection receive the reveal Monday morning instead. Salesforce handles eligibility evaluation, branching logic, message delivery, and redemption tracking from start to finish.
Use Case 5 — 8 & Donate Rewards Journey
Members collect stamps with every burger. At stamps 1, 5, 7, and 8, progressive communications build anticipation — culminating in a donation to the member's locally nominated charity through Grill'd's Local Matters program. Salesforce detects each milestone in real time and delivers the right message, personalised with the charity name, at every stage.

Solution Architecture
Five layers — from POS event to member message

The Salesforce platform for Grill'd operates across five layers. TASK POS and the AFL API generate real-time events that flow through Data 360 for unification and processing, into Marketing Cloud (Next) for journey orchestration, and out to the member's device. Every layer is configurable via API, giving Grill'd's development team full control without UI dependency.

Use Case 5
8 & Donate Rewards Journey

Members collect stamps with every burger. Salesforce detects stamp milestones in real time — triggering progressive communications at stamps 1, 5, 7, and 8. At stamp 8, TASK processes the donation to the member's locally nominated charity through Local Matters. A re-engagement flow activates if the member goes quiet before completing their cycle.

Customer Experience
Headless Execution
Stamp 1 — Welcome
Member scans their first burger at POS. A welcome message arrives in the app: "Welcome to 8 & Donate — your burgers now support [Charity Name]. Here's how it works."
1
TASK → D360 stamp event
TASK fires stamp_scan with stamp_number: 1 to D360 Streaming API. Streaming Insight detects milestone. Data Action fires. MCA Welcome Flow triggered with charityName from Unified Profile.
TASKD360 SIMCA Flow
Stamp 5 — Halfway
"5 down, 3 to go. Your next three burgers help [Charity Name] — keep going." Member sees their progress and the charity name in a single glance.
2
Milestone detection — stamp 5
Streaming Insight detects stamp_number = 5. Data Action triggers Halfway Flow. Transactional Push personalised with stampsRemaining: 3 and charityName from D360 Unified Profile.
D360 SITransact Push
Stamp 7 — One more
"One more burger and [Charity Name] gets a donation. You're almost there." Urgency and emotional connection in a single message.
3
Milestone detection — stamp 7
Same pattern. stamp_number = 7 Streaming Insight fires Data Action → One More Flow. Charity name passed as flow variable to MCA message definition.
D360 DAMCA Flow
Stamp 8 — Donation confirmed
"You did it! Your donation to [Charity Name] is confirmed. Thank you." Push + email arrives with charity name, donation amount, and a receipt. The Local Matters moment.
4
Donation moment flow
Streaming Insight detects stamp_number = 8. Donation Moment Flow fires. TASK processes the charity donation. TASK sends donation confirmation event back to D360. MCA sends celebratory push + email with charityName and donation amount.
TASK DonationD360Push + Email
Re-engagement — if member goes quiet
If no stamp scan in the configured window, a reminder arrives: "Your [Charity Name] is waiting. Come back and finish what you started."
5
Inactivity Streaming Insight
D360 Streaming Insight monitors last_stamp_date against a configured inactivity window. When threshold is breached, Data Action triggers Re-engagement Flow. Message personalised with charity name and stamps remaining.
D360 SIRe-engage Flow

Middleware may not be required for the core ingestion path, subject to TASK endpoint configuration and operational requirements such as auth, retries, and schema mapping.
