Why Now
Four shifts that make this
the moment to act on loyalty
Retail loyalty is at an inflection point. The tools to personalise at scale exist — what's been missing is the platform to connect them without a connector tax.
Loyalty data is siloed from service
When a Beauty Club member calls about an order, the service agent sees the transaction — not the loyalty tier, the recent offer history, or the churn risk score. That gap costs Priceline every contact where the right retention offer wasn't surfaced.
AI can now act on loyalty context
Agentforce agents reason across a member's full profile — tier, offer history, product affinity, churn signal — and issue the right offer autonomously. Not a static rule. A reasoned, real-time decision made without a human in the loop.
Offer complexity is outgrowing the tools
Beauty Club runs tiered discounts, partner earn schemes, gamified challenges, and seasonal promotions simultaneously. Basket adjudication — stacking, highest-discount, first-promotion — can't be managed manually at 6M member scale.
Competitors are already moving
Woolworths Everyday Rewards and Chemist Warehouse have both invested in real-time personalisation infrastructure. Every quarter without a unified offer engine widens the gap — in member satisfaction and in lifetime value captured per member.

01
Data Cloud
Unified member profile
Ingest from every touchpoint — in-store transactions, app behaviour, web browsing, service interactions, partner earn events. Identity resolution builds one real-time Beauty Club member profile. No nightly batch. A plan upgrade triggers an immediate offer eligibility recalculation.
02
Loyalty Management
Program & offer engine
Full-lifecycle program management — tier design, offer catalogue, vouchers, gamification, basket adjudication, partner ecosystem. Built natively on Salesforce CRM. Includes the telco/retail industry template. Agentforce is included in all editions.
03
Agentforce + Einstein
AI decisioning
Autonomous offer actions — agents issue bonus credits, retention offers, and personalised promotions based on real-time LTV and churn signals. Einstein predicts member engagement score, next best offer, and promotion revenue. No ML engineering required.
04
Service Cloud
Agent desktop
Loyalty Member Profile widget inline — every service agent sees tier, offer history, current eligibility, and LTV score before they say hello. Agentforce recommends the next best offer as part of complaint resolution. One click to apply — case closed with offer attached.
05
Marketing Cloud Adv.
Journey orchestration
Loyalty milestone triggers fire Marketing Cloud journeys natively — tier upgrade, offer expiry alert, churn risk intervention. SMS, push, email, and in-app — all from a single journey definition. No connector between Loyalty Management and Marketing Cloud.
06
Revenue Cloud
Order & offer capture
Loyalty tier and offer eligibility evaluated natively at quote time — Revenue Cloud reads loyalty status directly from the shared data layer. Eligible offers surface inline at order capture. Configured once in Loyalty Management, available in Revenue Cloud, Service Cloud, and digital channels simultaneously.
