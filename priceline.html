The Connected Stack
Six layers. One engine.
Built on data Priceline already owns.
Each layer activates the one above it. Nothing here is net-new infrastructure — this reuses what's already in flight.
6 layers
Priceline Beauty Club — Personalisation Engine powered by Salesforce
01
Data Cloud
Unified Member Profile
Ingests transaction history, app events, in-store POS data, and web behaviour — and resolves it into a single real-time profile per Beauty Club member. This is the source of truth everything else reads from.
02
Einstein Studio
Propensity & Churn Scoring
Custom models score every member for category propensity, next-best-product, and early churn signals — refreshed on each new event. Sub-100ms inference means the score is live when the member is live.
03
Global Promotions Mgmt
Offer Eligibility Engine
Promotion rules — bonus points eligibility, offer stacking logic, suppression for recent redeemers — evaluated in real time against the live member profile. Marketing owns the rules. Engineering doesn't need to touch them.
04
Loyalty Management
Points & Tier Engine
Beauty Club points, tier progression, and Sister Club card rewards managed as a single source — with real-time balance updates visible across app, in-store, and web. No more end-of-night batch point accruals.
05
Marketing Cloud Next
Email · Push · In-App · SMS
Executes the triggered communication — email, push, SMS, or in-app card — at the moment the decision fires. One canvas controls all four channels from a single trigger event.
06
Adaptive Web
Homepage & App Personalisation
Einstein Next-Best-Action drives the Priceline app homepage — surfacing personalised product carousels and offers to each member based on their live category mix. No two members see the same homepage.

Early Churn Detection
Re-activate members before they go quiet
Phase 1
Declining visit frequency
Einstein flags members whose in-store or app visit frequency drops 40%+ over 21 days — triggering a personalised bonus-points incentive while the relationship is still warm, not a generic voucher 90 days later.
Points expiry re-engagement
14 days before a member's points expire, fire a personalised "your points are expiring" message with a curated product recommendation based on their last purchase category — converting a churn risk into a visit.

Sister Club card personalisation
